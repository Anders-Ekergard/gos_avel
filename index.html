Gös candidate-gene dossier

Screens a gene symbol against UniProt, Ensembl, NCBI SRA/GEO and EVA for pikeperch (Sander lucioperca) and Nile tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus, a phylogenetic bridge species closer to pikeperch than the zebrafish most fish functional-genetics literature is based on) - to see what's already publicly known before planning any wet-lab work or synteny analysis. Part of an in-silico pikeperch breeding pipeline (candidate genes → marker/phenotype association → GRM/OCS breeding-value optimization); this page covers the candidate-gene step. Source: candidate_gene_dossier.py.

Try socs1a, mstnb, igf1, igf2, or ghr - all already confirmed to exist for pikeperch.

Breeding pipeline: marker association → breeding value → GRM → OCS pairing

Paste or edit genotype (djur_id + marker columns, genotypes coded AA/AB/BB) and phenotype (djur_id + trait column) CSVs. Runs the full chain: additive marker-effect estimation, per-individual breeding value, pairwise genomic similarity (a simple identity-by-state GRM proxy), OCS-style pairing that avoids the most related candidates, and a multi-generation inbreeding projection. Source: marker_fenotyp_koppling.py → avelsvarde.py → ocs.py.

Toy data preloaded below (20 individuals, 3 markers, feed-conversion-ratio phenotype) - illustrates the method, not a claim about real pikeperch. Paste your own data to try it on something else.
